# Returns Policy
You can return items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, undamaged, and in original packaging.
Return shipping: free for EU customers using our prepaid label. UK and USA customers pay EUR 9 return shipping, deducted from the refund.
Non-returnable items: engraved or custom items, rings that we resized, gift cards, and items from the Final Sale category.
To start a return, email support with your order number. Do not send items back without a return confirmation.
