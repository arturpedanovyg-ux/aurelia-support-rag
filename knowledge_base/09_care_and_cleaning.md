# Jewelry Care
Remove jewelry before swimming, showering, sports, and cleaning. Avoid contact with perfume, lotion and chlorine.
Silver: polish with a soft silver cloth. Silver naturally tarnishes over time; store it in the pouch we provide, away from air and moisture.
Vermeil: wipe with a soft dry cloth only. Do not use polishing cloths, abrasive cleaners, or ultrasonic cleaners, as they remove the plating.
Solid Gold: can be cleaned with lukewarm water, mild soap and a soft brush.
