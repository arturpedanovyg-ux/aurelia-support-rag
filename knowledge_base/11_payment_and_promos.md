# Payment and Promotions
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Klarna (pay in 3 installments) is available in the EU and UK only.
Prices are in EUR. EU prices include VAT. US customers may be charged by their bank in USD at the bank's exchange rate.
Promo codes: one code per order, and codes cannot be used on Sale or Final Sale items. New newsletter subscribers get 10% off their first order.
Promo codes cannot be applied after an order is placed.
