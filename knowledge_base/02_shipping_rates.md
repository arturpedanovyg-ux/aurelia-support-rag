# Shipping Destinations and Rates
We ship only to the EU, the United Kingdom and the USA. We do not ship to other countries.
Standard shipping:
- EU: EUR 4.90, free for orders over EUR 80. Delivery 3-6 business days.
- UK: EUR 9.90, free for orders over EUR 150. Delivery 5-8 business days.
- USA: EUR 14.90, free for orders over EUR 200. Delivery 7-12 business days.
Express shipping (EU only): EUR 14.90, delivery 1-3 business days. Express is never free.
Customers in the UK and USA are responsible for import duties and taxes charged by customs.
