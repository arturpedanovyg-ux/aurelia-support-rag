# Ring Sizing Guide
Our rings are sold in EU sizes 46-64. Approximate US equivalents: EU 52 = US 6, EU 54 = US 7, EU 57 = US 8.
To measure at home, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark the overlap, measure the length in millimetres and divide by 3.14 to get the diameter. Our printable size chart is on each ring page.
If between sizes, choose the larger one. We do not offer resizing as a standalone service; use the free size exchange instead.
