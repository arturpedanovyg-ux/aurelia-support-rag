# Warranty
Silver and Solid Gold pieces have a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects (loose stones, broken clasps, faulty soldering).
Vermeil (gold-plated) pieces have a 6-month warranty on the plating.
The warranty does not cover normal wear, scratches, loss, damage from accidents, or damage from chemicals, perfume or chlorine.
To claim, email support with your order number and clear photos. We answer within 2 business days. Approved repairs or replacements are free, and shipping is covered within the EU.
