# About Aurelia Jewelry and Contact
Aurelia Jewelry is an online-only jewelry store. We have no physical shops. Orders ship from our warehouse in Poland.
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 CET, by email (support@aurelia.example) and live chat on our website. We reply to emails within 24 hours on business days. We do not offer phone support.
Collections: Silver (925 sterling silver), Vermeil (925 silver with 18k gold plating), and Solid Gold (14k).
