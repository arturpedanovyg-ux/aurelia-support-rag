# Order Processing and Tracking
Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later or on weekends ship the next business day.
Engraved or custom items need 3-5 additional business days of processing before shipping.
When an order ships, you receive an email with a tracking number. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear.
If a package shows no movement for 7 business days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
