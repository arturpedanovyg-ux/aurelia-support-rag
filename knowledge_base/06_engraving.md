# Engraving Service
Engraving costs EUR 15 per item. Rings allow up to 12 characters, pendants and bracelets up to 20 characters.
Allowed: letters, numbers, and the symbols . - / and a heart. Emojis, logos and custom fonts are not available.
Engraving adds 3-5 business days to processing.
Engraved items cannot be returned or exchanged, except if they are defective. You can cancel an engraved order only within 12 hours after placing it.
