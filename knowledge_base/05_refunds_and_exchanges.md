# Refunds and Exchanges
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-10 business days after we receive and inspect the return.
Original shipping costs are refunded only if the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.
Size exchange: each non-engraved ring can be exchanged once for another size, free of charge, within 30 days of delivery. Exchange shipping is free within the EU.
Exchanges for a different product are treated as a return plus a new order.
